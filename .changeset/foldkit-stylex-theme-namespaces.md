Each component module exports one namespace named after the component, such as `Listbox` from `@kutur-kit/foldkit-stylex-theme/listbox`: @foldkit/ui's module with the member that makes the view (`view`, `create`, Toast's `make` or DragAndDrop's `draggable`) styled by the theme, `restyle` to layer your styles over the theme, and helpers that draw marks inside your elements, such as `Listbox.buttonContent`, `Checkbox.mark` and `Dialog.closeIcon`. The 28 `*Theme` looks are gone, and so are the indicators such as `checkboxIndicator`, `listboxButton`, `selectChevron` and `switchThumb`. `@foldkit/ui`, `foldkit` and `effect` are now peers. When upgrading, import the namespace in place of @foldkit/ui's module and the look, and call `Checkbox.view` or `Listbox.create()` instead of wrapping the view with the core. Replace a look spread into your styles with `X.restyle(layer)`, and drop a property the theme sets with `null` in that layer. Render the helpers where you rendered the indicators or your own `"▾"` and `"✓"`; the switch draws its thumb itself, so render its button empty.

The look is neutral: `color.accent` defaults to `color.text` and `color.onAccent` to `color.surface`, and the greys `color.subtle`, `color.border`, `color.control` and `color.muted` mix `color.text` into `color.surface` in OKLab, so a preset that sets those two gets matching greys. A part that sets `color.surface` or `color.text` for itself sets the four greys, plus `color.accent` and `color.onAccent`, too. Edges are 1px, focus rings use `color.accent`, hover styles apply only under `@media (hover: hover)`, and under reduced motion parts still fade but no longer move. `color.hover` is renamed `color.subtle`; `color.focus`, `size.box`, `size.thumb` and `size.arrow` are gone, replaced by `color.accent` and the new `size.mark`; `radius.panel` follows `radius.control` unless set.
