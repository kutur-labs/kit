`@kutur-kit/foldkit-stylex/testing` checks a page in Chromium for what axe-core leaves out. `audit` reports control edges whose most contrasting border, fill or shown mark stays below 3:1, keyboard focus that does not show, is covered or shows only as an outline or box-shadow ring below 3:1, targets under 24px other than links in a line of text, sideways scrolling, fixed boxes or their content past the viewport, cut-off text with and without WCAG 1.4.12's spacing, movement under reduced motion and states lost under forced colors, each finding with what to change. At `level: "AA"`, targets under 24px pass when spaced as WCAG 2.5.8 allows. The default `kit` level adds 44px targets under a coarse pointer, 2px focus outlines, focus changes it cannot measure and movement. An edge, mark or focus ring over an image or gradient gives an `unmeasured` finding, to check by eye. `{ hairline }` names fields that rest at a faint edge on a fill of their own, which the edge rule then asks for that fill, at least 1.05:1, outside forced colors. `recordMotion` logs CSS transitions and animations longer than a frame for it, and each audit takes only the records in its own scope. `variations` lists the browser contexts to run it in, a 320px touch phone included, as Vitest browser instances or Playwright projects. Both functions are self-contained, so Playwright can send them to the page.

Under Vitest, `foldkitStylex()` now makes the compiled modules insert their CSS and keeps both packages out of pre-bundling, so browser tests measure the styled page without extra options, installed packages included.
