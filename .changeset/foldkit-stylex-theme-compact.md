A denser look. Controls are `size.control` tall, 32px, with `size.target` at 24px under a fine pointer; both are at least 44px under a coarse pointer, whatever an app-wide override sets. Type steps down to `fontSize.xs` 12px through `fontSize.lg` 16px, with new `lineHeight` and `fontWeight` groups, and `space` gains `xl`. Fields, triggers and buttons rest at a `color.border` hairline on the `color.subtle` fill and turn the edge to `color.control` on hover; a read-only field is a well, `color.text` on a mix of 8% `color.text` into `color.surface` that no token names. Panels draw their edge with `shadow.panel`'s ring, in `color.border`, and a border only under forced colors, and buttons rise on the new `shadow.control`. A primary button's hover moves its fill away from `color.surface`, so its label keeps its contrast. `motion.instant` times hover colors and a button's press, highlights appear at once and fade out, and panels open from a scale of 0.97 and leave faster than they enter. Marks are `size.mark` square. The greys mix less: `color.subtle` 5% and `color.border` 10%, and the dark surface and text are `#111113` and `#ededed`.

Components follow: Listbox, Combobox and DatePicker share a 12rem width and trigger padding, Fieldset heads its group with the legend instead of a frame, Tabs and Nav keep 44px targets under a finger, a disabled calendar day is struck through only under forced colors, the Switch's track is `color.control` while off, drag-and-drop lists grow with their items, and Dialog, Toast and Tooltip take the new type and timing. A trigger's label ends in an ellipsis rather than pushing out its mark.

Under forced colors, a primary button, the active list or menu item, Nav's current link and the selected calendar day opt out of forced color adjustment, so their HighlightText labels show on the Highlight fill instead of on Chromium's Canvas backplate, which hid them. Under a coarse pointer, the DatePicker panel keeps 1px of inline padding, so its 44px days fit a 320px screen.
